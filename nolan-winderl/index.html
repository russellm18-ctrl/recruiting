ABOUT NOLAN

Nolan Winderl is a Class of 2029 distance runner from Peninsula High School in Washington, competing for the Seahawks in cross country and track and field. He holds personal bests across six events, from the 800m to the 5K, led by a 4:33.37 1600m at the South Sound Classic and a 2:04.27 800m at the Puget Sound League Championships.

This fall he ran a 10:45.9 two-mile at the Olympia XC Classic and a 15:53.4 5K personal best at the Hoka John Payne Invitational at Chambers Bay. He carries a 3.72 GPA.

In his words“I am a hardworking distance runner who prioritizes a team-first mindset. Driven by collective success, I constantly look for ways to improve throughout the season and beyond. Academically, I challenge myself with high-level classes and always strive to put my best foot forward.”
